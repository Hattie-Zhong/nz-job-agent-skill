# Match score (100 points)

Score only fit. Eligibility is a separate gate and is never part of the score.
A high score never overrides a failed eligibility check. Do not invent
percentages or probabilities outside this framework. Tell the user honestly
when a role is a weak fit.

For each category, pick the band first, then the exact score inside it.

## Technical match (30)
- 25 to 30: nearly all important technical requirements backed by direct project or work evidence
- 18 to 24: most requirements met, some only partial or indirect
- 10 to 17: meaningful overlap, but several important capabilities missing or weakly evidenced
- 0 to 9: little relevant overlap

## Domain / business match (20)
- 17 to 20: direct experience in this industry or business problem
- 12 to 16: closely related industry or strong transferable business knowledge
- 7 to 11: some general business relevance
- 0 to 6: little domain relevance

## Project evidence (20)
- 17 to 20: at least one project directly addresses the role's real problem, with strong verified evidence
- 12 to 16: relevant project with good evidence, but only partly matching the problem
- 7 to 11: projects show general skills but not this problem
- 0 to 6: no relevant project evidence

## Professional / transferable experience (15)
- 13 to 15: work experience directly relevant to the role's duties
- 9 to 12: strong transferable experience with clear links
- 5 to 8: some transferable experience
- 0 to 4: little relevant experience

## Role-specific fit (15)
- 13 to 15: the profile closely matches the candidate persona and the business problem
- 9 to 12: good match with some gaps
- 5 to 8: partial match
- 0 to 4: weak match

## Classification and recommendation

| Score | Classification | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| 85 to 100 | VERY STRONG MATCH | PROCEED |
| 70 to 84 | STRONG MATCH | PROCEED unless there is a significant screening risk |
| 55 to 69 | POSSIBLE / STRETCH | CONSIDER |
| below 55 | LOW MATCH | SKIP (do not write a CV or letter unless the user overrides) |

Every band goes to Checkpoint 1. The recommendation is advice for the user,
never permission to continue.
