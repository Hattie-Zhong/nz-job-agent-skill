# Default writing rules

These apply to every user. The user's own `profile/writing_rules.md` adds to
these and wins if they conflict. Limits (pages, words) come from `config.yaml`.

## Both documents
- NZ English spelling.
- Plain, natural English at the user's own level. No fancy vocabulary.
- Avoid phrases that sound machine-written, for example: "I am excited to",
  "passionate about leveraging", "proven track record", "in today's fast-paced
  world", "I am confident that I would be a great fit", "delve", "synergy".
- No dashes used as punctuation (em dash, en dash, or a spaced hyphen) when
  `style.no_dashes_in_cover_letter` is true. Hyphenated words such as
  "part-time" are fine.
- Never state total years of experience or anything that reveals age unless
  the user's rules allow it.
- Use full names, not course codes or internal project names a recruiter
  would not understand.
- No Markdown or placeholders left in the final text.

## CV
- Within the page limit in `config.yaml`.
- No photo.
- Start each bullet with a strong past-tense verb (present tense for the current role).
- One idea per bullet. Numbers only if they are recorded in the profile.
- No bold or italic inside bullets or the summary. Bold is only for job titles,
  qualification names and project names on their heading lines.
- Write the CV summary without "I", "my" or "me" (for example "Master of Data
  Science student with ...", not "I am a student ..."). The cover letter uses "I" as normal.
- Each fact appears once only across the whole CV.

## Cover letter
- Within the word limit in `config.yaml` and one page.
- Do not use the generic structure "who I am, what I study, my skills, thanks".
  Follow the approved paragraph plan.
- Each paragraph links the user's evidence to this employer's needs.
- Every duty or story in the letter must also appear under the same employer in the CV.
